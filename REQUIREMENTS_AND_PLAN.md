# RF2027 CSC — Proposal Requirements & Suggested Plan

Source: two screenshots of the kit's required proposal structure, cross-checked against the kit PDF.

## A. Required paper structure (8 parts)

1. **Title Page** — Title; School Name and Team Name; Name of Participants; Date Submitted.
2. **Introduction to the Proposed Approach for Reducing Waterbody Pollution in the Philippines** — Target site profile & root-cause diagnosis.
3. **Suggested process/product** for the approach — Plan of Action; Raw Materials to be used (if any); Steps for Production/Theoretical Implementation; Efficiency of the process/product.
4. **Discussion of the expected end output and its function(s)** — see deliverable 4, kit pp. 10–11.
5. **Feasibility and Economic Analysis** — Overall Plan (production requirements, facilities, marketing strategy, if any); Estimated Costs; Technical Feasibility (does the country have the existing technology?); National scaling blueprint.
6. **Implications** — Health and Environment; Community Involvement.
7. **References** — APA format.
8. **Declaration Form for Using Artificial Intelligence (AI)**.

## B. Paper format

- Limited to **2,200 words** (excluding References).
- **Times New Roman 12** · Letter 8.5" × 11" · **1-inch margins on all sides** · line spacing **1.5**.

## C. Digital poster

- Contains the title and the summarized, simplified content of the study.
- **3:4 aspect ratio, portrait layout.**

Deadline Dec 5, 2026, 11:59 PM PHT → rf.ysc@upalchemes.org, filenames per kit (`YSC Phase I_[School]_[Team#].pdf`, `YSC Poster_[School]_[Team#].png/.jpg`).
