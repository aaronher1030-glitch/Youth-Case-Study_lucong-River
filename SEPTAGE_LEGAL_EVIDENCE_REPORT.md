# Household Septage & Septic-Tank Management, Manila — Legal Evidence Report

13 Nov 2026. **[VERIFIED]** primary text read; **[PARTIAL]** incomplete/secondary; **[UNVERIFIED]** unconfirmed. Dates = issuance.

**§1. PD 856 (1976).** Ch. XVII s. 75: tanks "shall not be constructed under any building and within 25 meters from any source of water supply"; s. 76: effluent to a "sub-surface soil, absorption field" or "purification device", to a stream only "if it conforms to the quality standards". https://lawplayer.com/ph/article/pd-856/s0075. **[VERIFIED]** — **Correction:** s. 75 has *no* manhole-access clause (contrary to a common secondary claim), and PD 856 sets **no desludging frequency**. **[VERIFIED]**

**§2. Revised IRR, Ch. XVII (27 May 2021).** Covers "desludging, collection, handling and transport, treatment, reuse, and disposal" and "applies to all local government units"; §2.5 defines desludging. https://legaldex.com/laws/revised-implementing-rules-and-regulations-of-chapter-xvii-of-the. **[VERIFIED]** §10.1.1(a): the Environmental Sanitation Clearance is "a prerequisite to the issuance of the Sanitary Permit"; §10.1.3(b): NCR applications go "directly to the DOH NCR"; §10.3.1: no discharge without a Local Health Office operational permit. §11.5.4–11.5.7: provider training, certificate of desludging, quarterly household list to DOH Regional Offices, SMR to DENR copied to the LHO. §11.6.1(b): EMB verifies ESCs before ECC and discharge permits. **[VERIFIED]**

**§3. RA 9275 (2004).** s. 7: DPWH prepares the NSSMP; "Each LGU shall appropriate the necessary land"; LGU may fund O&M via "local property taxes and enforcement of a service fee system". s. 8: concessionaires connect HUC sources; DOH issues collection/treatment guidelines. s. 14: discharge permit. s. 19: DENR sets "effluent standards" and classifies water bodies. s. 27: operating without a permit and "unauthorized transport or dumping … of sewage sludge" are prohibited. https://lawplayer.com/ph/act/ra-9275. **[VERIFIED]**

**§4. DENR-EMB permits, limits.** DAO 2005-10 Rule 3.1: standards apply to "the transport and disposal of effluent, sewage and septage offsite"; Rule 14.1 EMB Regional Office permit; 14.9 five-year validity; 14.15.1 24-hour breakdown notice. https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/phi202634.pdf (2005). **[VERIFIED]** DAO 2016-08 Table 1, Class C: BOD 50, COD 100, TSS 100, ammonia 0.5, total coliform 10,000 MPN/100 mL, fecal coliform 400 MPN/100 mL. https://emb.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/DAO-2016-08-WQS.pdf (2016). **[PARTIAL]** — primary PDF is image-only; figures secondary. These govern an outfall to the Class C Pasig. **[PARTIAL]**

**§5. DPWH NSSMP.** Authorised by RA 9275 s. 7 and DAO 2005-10 Rule 7. **[VERIFIED]** NSSMP-POM 2026 (DPWH D.O., 25 Mar 2026): subsidy 40% (NEDA Bd. Res. 3, s. 2012) → 50% (Res. 6, s. 2017) → "varies depending on proponent category" (Res. 41, s. 2024); "as of 2025, only two LGUs … secured subsidy approvals". https://www.dpwh.gov.ph/DPWH/sites/default/files/nssmp_program_operations_manual.pdf. **[VERIFIED]** **Material gap:** designed "for local government units … outside Metro Manila"; goals now sit in the PWSSMP (NEDA, 2020) — "universal access to adequate and equitable sanitation by 2030". Manila has **no clear NSSMP subsidy pathway**. **[VERIFIED quote / PARTIAL eligibility]**

**§6. Responsibility — the genuine ambiguity.** Households: Ord. 8491 s. 6; A.O. 2019-0047, Roles F(c), "regularly desludged within the designed cycle period". LGU: RIRR §11.4.1 (master plan; ordinances; "Local Sustainable Sanitation Plan which includes sewage and septage management program"), §11.4.3 (permits, revocation); RA 9275 s. 7. Concessionaire: Ord. 8491 s. 11 — "desludged and treated once every five (5) years for free by the water concessionaries"; RA 9275 s. 8. **[VERIFIED]** **Conflict unresolved:** 3–5 years (Ord. s. 6; A.O. VI.A.9) vs. **4 years** (A.O. VI.C.1, "must be desludged every 4 years") vs. 5 years free (Ord. s. 11) vs. "reduced the liquid capacity by 50%" (NSSMP-POM quoting PD 856). No statute designates one accountable entity. **[VERIFIED conflict / UNVERIFIED resolution]**

**§7. DOH vs. DENR vs. LGU.** DOH: standards and ESC, which gates the sanitary permit (RIRR §10.1.1, §11.4.3(e)). DENR-EMB: discharge permit, effluent standards, SMR, PCO accreditation (§11.5.10, §11.6.1). LGU: CENRO accredits desludgers "including … securing an Environmental Sanitation Clearance" (Ord. s. 10(c)); tank opening by sanitary inspector or authorised barangay officials (s. 6). Three approvals are needed — ESC, City accreditation, EMB permit — with no single window. **[VERIFIED as text / PARTIAL as practice]**

**§8. Manila ordinance.** Ordinance No. 8491 (enacted 17 Mar 2016; approved 17 May 2016). https://citycouncilofmanila.com.ph/city_ordinances/ordinance-no-8491-2/ **[VERIFIED]** s. 6 desludging and inspection; s. 8 accredited hauler only, no dumping; s. 9 ESC for permits; s. 10 CENRO accreditation; s. 14 refusal to desludge prohibited; s. 15 penalties. No barangay-level septage ordinance located. **[UNVERIFIED — absence only]**

**§9. Ownership and transport.** Septage = "liquid, solid, and semisolid material … which must be pumped, hauled, treated, and disposed of properly" (A.O. 2019-0047, def. 10; https://storage.googleapis.com/request-attachments/6EwhuQ4ZOXaXjFRmqv65lmCcYdYZN6NRRd2HNqo0gR6POY9BSW0vuoao4z3XxwahsBPYUzfxFcILqJH1tzpXhtFYjPzy91Hpxwm8/ao2019-0047.pdf, 29 Oct 2019). **[VERIFIED]** Transport only by an ESC-accredited provider under an MoA with a permitted STP (RIRR §10.1.2(e)), with a certificate of desludging on request (§11.5.5); Ord. 8491 s. 8. **[VERIFIED]** Nothing vests ownership in the household; custody passes at the manhole. **[PARTIAL — inference from silence]**

**§10. Students and volunteers.** RA 11058 covers "all establishments, projects, sites … except in the public sector" and protects employees: s. 4(a)(1),(5) duties; s. 8 free PPE; s. 6 right to refuse unsafe work; s. 21 joint and solidary liability. https://lawplayer.com/ph/act/ra-11058 (17 Aug 2018). **[VERIFIED]** No OSH statute extends to student volunteers; sanitation law requires only provider training (§11.5.4) and a Health and Safety Management Plan (§10.1.2(c)). **[VERIFIED]** Students must not open tanks or handle septage; no express ban exists. **[VERIFIED gap / UNVERIFIED prohibition]**
