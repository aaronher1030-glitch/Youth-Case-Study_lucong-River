## Deliberate omissions and why

- **No claim that Concepcion lacks piggery siting rules.** The project holds Concepcion's Citizen's Charter, not its zoning ordinance. The paper says only that Bamban *has* a schedule, which is verifiable.
- **No "Bamban farms" identification.** The Bamban CLUP confirms farmland on the shared boundary but names no farms. P14 stays open; the paper does not lean on it.
- **No compost revenue.** Untested market; leaving it out keeps the budget honest.

## Builder notes

`build_docx.py` gained four things this round, all backward compatible:

- optional `header_text` and `title` arguments (argv 3 and 4)
- ordered-list support (`1. ` lines render as `List Number`; previously they collapsed into one paragraph)
- `keep_with_next` on `**Table N.**` captions, so a caption never orphans from its table
- reference detection by `\d+\.\s*References`, with left alignment and a 0.5-inch hanging indent

**Markdown convention: leave one blank line between reference entries.** The parser joins consecutive non-blank lines into one paragraph, so a missing blank line silently merges two citations.

### Typography, 22 November

Body, headings and references are all 12 pt; both tables are 9.6 pt so six columns fit the 6.5-inch text block; the running header and figure caption are 9 pt; the title is 18 pt; the title-page lines are 11 pt. References at 12 pt no longer fit under §6 on page 7, so the last entries run onto page 8 and §8 follows them there. That is normal reference-wrapping, and uniform 12 pt is worth more to a format check than a tidy break.

Figure 1 is set at 5.8 inches wide, trimmed to its ink so no blank margin sits inside the image frame, and placed directly under its first mention on page 1. The previous arrangement — figure on page 3, caption orphaned on page 2 — came from the PNG's white border, not from the text.

The budget table now carries a `**Table 2.**` caption and sits immediately after the "Estimated costs" lead-in, with "Overall plan." following it. That ordering is what keeps all sixteen rows and the total on one page: with the plan paragraph first, the table started too low and jumped to the next page, leaving half of page 5 blank.
