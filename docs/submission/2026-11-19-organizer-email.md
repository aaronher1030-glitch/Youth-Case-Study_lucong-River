## Notes for the team

- Send from a school or team address if possible, not a personal one. It reads as an institutional query rather than an individual admission.
- Keep the reply. If the answer is favourable, attach it to the submission email so the declaration cannot later be read as concealment. If it is unfavourable, you have a written record and 16 days to change course.
- Do not ask "is it okay if we used AI a little." Ask what is permitted. The second is answerable; the first invites suspicion.
- If no reply by 28 November, send one short follow-up and copy the competition address again. Silent non-response is not permission.
- The 28 November date leaves a week of buffer before 5 December. Do not let this slide to the deadline.
