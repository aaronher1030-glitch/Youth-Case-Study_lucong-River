## Additions made on 20 November (rubric-driven)

The rubric in `schedule/_kit_text.txt` (pp. 17–19) grades **Environment 10%** on "any adverse effects on the environment and … how these can be overcome", and **Health 5%** on "adverse effects on the health and safety of the workers and surrounding community". Neither had any text. Both were scoring zero.

**Judgement call on the novelty paragraph.** The rubric's Ingenuity criterion asks for "resources and/or strategies that have not been utilized in the country". The honest position is that covered lagoons are not novel here — §5 says so plainly, which is right for Practicality. The paragraph therefore relocates novelty to the institutional design and does not claim no LGU has ever tried it. If a judge presses for a citation of novelty, there isn't one, and the paper should not pretend otherwise.

## Highest-risk sentences in the paper

Ranked by how badly they fail if challenged:

1. **"No compost revenue is counted"** — safe, but invites "then who pays in year two?" Have an answer ready: the revolving fund and the permit condition.
2. **Unit costs with no quotation** — item 34.
3. **"No new ordinance is needed"** — item 31. True for a pilot, arguable at scale.
4. **Piggeries as main cause** — correctly labelled as a hypothesis under test, which is the right posture.
5. **"Neither barangay has a sewer network"** — item 15. Easy to confirm, embarrassing if wrong.
