# Waterway Source-Control Pilot Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** This is a conditional research/operations plan, not authorization for field implementation. Any subsequently authorized agentic document or analysis work should use `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` or `superpowers:executing-plans` task by task. Fieldwork, procurement, agency contact, submission and declarations require separate human authorization and accountable professional supervision. Checkboxes below describe future work; they do not imply completion.

**Goal:** Establish whether a one-kilometer household-waste source-control pilot can be delivered safely, evaluated honestly and sustained within the Research Fair 2027 ₱10 million, twelve-month constraints.

**Architecture:** A single primary Stream 1 service joins household segregation/storage, dependable collection, lawful covered transfer and verified receiving-facility delivery. Independent observations assess service additionality and local litter outcomes. Estero de Paco is the first site to verify; no exact reach has been selected.

**Tech stack:** Standard GIS for mapping; spreadsheet/CSV records with a data dictionary; calibrated weighing equipment; repeatable bank-based observations; method-appropriate recognized laboratory services. No custom app, AI model, IoT system, automated drain closure or river barrier is required.

**Spec and evidence:** [Research dossier](../../../DEEP_RESEARCH_RF2027_WATERWAY_FEASIBILITY.md). Numbered source citations in this plan refer to that dossier's bibliography. Governing document: [Research Fair 2027 CSC Kit](file:///C:/Users/Ma%20Christine/Downloads/Research-Fair-2027-CSC-Kit.pdf).

- National expansion blueprint must address **Pasig, Tullahan or Meycauayan**; this plan prioritizes Pasig. [1]
- Phase I deadline: **December 5, 2026, 11:59 PM PHT**. The pilot's twelve months are not a requirement to finish field implementation by that deadline. [1]

- No displacement, in-water student work, confined-space entry, hazardous-waste sorting, sediment disturbance or full-channel obstruction.
- Planned monitoring outside the reach is observation only, not an extension of physical implementation; confirm that interpretation with the organizers.
- Planning quantities and prices below are assumptions, inclusive of applicable supply/service charges where relevant; they are not vendor quotations. Actual payroll statutory costs must replace the provisional loading.

## 1. Decision and boundaries

**Proceed now:** desk research has been completed sufficiently to recommend a conditional service-first direction. Human review can consider site verification and organizer clarification.

**Do not proceed yet:** final competition authorship using this material, choosing an unsurveyed exact kilometer, promising performance, buying equipment, hiring crews, arranging visits or submitting anything.

The chosen concept is a **service reliability and verified-disposal pilot**, not a claim to restore all water quality. The mechanism to test is reduced locally generated litter leakage through improved service, not water purification. Historical Paco evidence includes sewage-related BOD; that problem is not solved by collecting solid waste. [2]

**Fallback:** investigate Estero de San Miguel only if Paco fails and sufficient time remains for the same checks. Do not transfer Paco assumptions or Tullahan measurements. If no candidate passes, stop the site-selection claim rather than inventing feasibility. [4,5]

## 6. Measurement definitions and decision thresholds

Every target below is proposed. Actual baseline cells are **not observed**, rather than zero.

| Measure | Definition | Proposed decision threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Household reach | Consenting eligible households actually served within approved footprint | Scenario 400, replaced by verified inventory before contracting |
| Service reliability | Completed household pickup opportunities by the agreed window ÷ all scheduled household pickup opportunities | ≥95% in the fixed months 4–12 window; also report every scheduled opportunity from actual rollout onward |
| Recovery from missed service | Missed household pickup opportunities resolved within 24 elapsed hours of the original window ÷ all missed opportunities | ≥90%; unsafe conditions never overridden, pauses remain visible in the unadjusted denominator |
| Documented disposition | Transferred mass with valid receiving evidence ÷ total transferred mass | 100% traceable; no double-counted transfers |
| Uncontrolled storage overflow | Recorded uncontrolled overflow/spill events | Target zero; every incident triggers corrective action |
| Primary environmental outcome | Standardized downstream-direction visible floating-litter passage rate at the fixed boundary | Provisional 20% reduction; baseline precision and controllable-source share must justify target before intervention |
| Safety | Serious incidents and unresolved unsafe conditions | No serious preventable injury; unsafe conditions stop affected work, not reporting |
| Sustainability | Authorized operator and recurring funding for continuation | Written decision before handover; otherwise funded safe closure |

**Data fields:** station/route ID; timestamp and timezone; observation duration; width/area observed; visible size threshold; litter category/count; flow direction; tide stage where relevant; rainfall/water level; observer ID; missingness reason; concurrent cleanup; household-service count; load ID; gross/tare/net mass; destination receipt ID; rejection/incident record.

**Analysis boundaries:** counts are items per stated observation duration/width, not tonnes. Reverse-flow observations remain separate. Monthly chemistry without reliable discharge estimates yields concentrations, not loads. No claim of disease prevention, drinking/bathing safety, metal removal or full river restoration is supported by this protocol. One treated reach does not become many independent treatment units because it has many observations. [2,4,6,11,12]

**Service counting rules:** freeze each household's scheduled frequency and collection window before rollout, using the accepted capacity plan. One scheduled household-window is one opportunity, even when several households share a route. A documented timely visit with no material set out is a completed opportunity; an unvisited household is not. Publish months 3–12 scheduled/delivered counts, enrollment and startup coverage alongside the fixed months 4–12 KPI. Weather-adjusted figures may be supplementary, never replacements for the unadjusted record; stopping unsafe work takes precedence over the target.

**Environmental decision rule:** before intervention, freeze the model, comparison strategy and covariate standardization. Estimate `R = 100 × (1 − μ_service / μ_no_service)`, where the means are modeled comparable downstream-direction visible-litter rates for the fixed months 4–12 evaluation window and `μ_no_service` is the explicitly modeled untreated counterfactual. This is not net river export. If the counterfactual is not defensible or is zero, do not report a percentage effect as identified. Use a 95% interval that accounts for temporal dependence. For the predeclared practical target `T` (provisionally 20%): **supported target** if the interval lower bound is ≥T; **evidence against achieving T** if its upper bound is <T; **inconclusive for T** otherwise. A result below T may still show some benefit; do not label it no effect. No empirical interval or detectable effect is claimed now.

**Success interpretation:** expansion requires the supported-target classification plus reliable service, acceptable safety and recurring affordability. Target/model amendments must occur before intervention on documented baseline grounds; no post-result lowering of T or shifting the evaluation window. Publish adverse, null and inconclusive findings alongside improvements.
