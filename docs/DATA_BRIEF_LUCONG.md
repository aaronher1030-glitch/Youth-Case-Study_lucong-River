**How to read the status column:** *Verified* means a held document states it. *Derived* means it is arithmetic on verified numbers. *Assumption* means the team chose it and Month 1 tests it. *Missing* means the paper needs it and does not have it.

## 2. Water quality

Class C standard for fecal coliform is 200 MPN/100 mL (DAO 2016-08). The Lucong reads **16,000**, which is **80 times** the standard. Every other parameter is inside the limit, which is why the paper argues fecal load rather than industrial effluent.

Source: EMB Region III, *Regional state of the brown environment report 2022*, October 2024 to 2025 monitoring round. **Status:** the values are verified as published. The provenance of the Lucong row (which station, which quarter, how many samples) is **Missing** — that is task P12.

| Reading | Value | What it means for the argument |

**Decision this drives:** whether the 16,000 figure survives scrutiny. If EMB cannot confirm the row, the paper has no headline number. That is why P12 outranks every other open item.

**The conflict you must resolve.** The paper's design handles 180 head (15 sheds × 12). One farm in the same municipality is reported at 10,000 head. Both numbers are true; they measure different populations. The paper's population is the small sheds on the reach's banks, not the municipality. A judge will still ask, so the answer must be ready in one sentence: *the service is sized to the banks of the reach, and the 10,000-head farm is 500 m from the town centre, not on this reach.*

**Decision this drives:** whether to keep the 10,000-head sentence. It is the strongest evidence that rules alone fail here. It is also the easiest number in the paper to turn against the design. Keeping it is the honest choice only if P8 lands.

**The gap the entry fills.** Every instrument here regulates the farm; none moves manure off it. Naga wrote the strictest rule in the set and amended it five years later because complaints continued. That is the novelty claim, and it is a claim about a documented absence, not about Filipino ingenuity in general.

**Decision this drives:** whether to adopt Bamban's schedule as a Concepcion recommendation. Do not claim Concepcion has no siting rules. The team does not hold Concepcion's zoning ordinance, so the absence is unproven. The defensible line is: Bamban has an adopted numeric schedule; Concepcion's ordinance has not been obtained.
